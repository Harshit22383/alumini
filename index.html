About AlumniHub

A dedicated student portal built for connecting generations of school alumni.

AlumniHub is an interactive, passout-exclusive platform designed to replace fragmented WhatsApp groups with a structured community space. Whether you are transitioning to university, looking for job referrals, or organizing your 10-year reunion, the hub keeps everyone connected.

🎓 Verified Network

Strictly for verified school passouts, ensuring high-trust discussions and networking.

⚡ Open Source & Scalable

Built using modern web standards and easily deployable directly to GitHub Pages.
